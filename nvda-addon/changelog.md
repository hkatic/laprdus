# Laprdus for NVDA — What's New

## Version 2.0.0

- Every voice speaks every language. Croatian, Serbian, Bosnian and Slovenian are chosen in a new Language setting, which comes before the Voice setting in the voice settings and in the synth settings ring, separately from the voice, as with synthesizer B: Josip can speak Serbian, Stojan Croatian. The language chooses the pronunciation rules, the accent dictionary, the letter names when spelling and the number words (*tisuća*, *milijun* in Croatian; *hiljada*, *milion* in Serbian and Bosnian). A new installation starts in NVDA's language when that is Croatian, Serbian or Bosnian, otherwise in Croatian; a language you have chosen is kept.
- Zvonko is the default voice, whatever the language. The voice list shows all eighteen voices for every language; a voice you have chosen yourself is never replaced.
- Automatic language switching: with NVDA's "Automatic language switching" on, text marked as Croatian, Serbian or Bosnian is spoken in that language, with the same voice; text in any other language is spoken in the chosen language.
- Three new voices synthesized entirely by rule, with no recordings: Zvonko, Stojan and Mirsad. They stay clear and intelligible at very high speech rates. Josip, Vlado, Detence, Baba and Đedo remain available.
- Bosnian and Slovenian are now supported languages.
- The add-on, its user guide and the Laprdus Configurator are available in English, Croatian, Serbian and Slovenian. All follow NVDA's language; a language without a translation gets English, except that the Configurator reads Croatian under Bosnian.
- The user guide opened from the Add-on Store is readable at last: its table of contents and cross-references work, and its tables and code examples display as tables and code.
- Some voices have new names and ids; a saved choice is carried over when the add-on is updated: Djed is now Đedo (id `djedo`), Klapa Zvonko is Dalmacija Klapa (`dalmacija_klapa`), Zvonko Pjevač is Lika Velebit (`lika_velebit`), Stojan Pevač is Solunac (`solunac`) and Mirsad Solist is Sarajevo Bembaša (`sarajevo_bembasa`); the older ids `dijete` and `baka` are Detence and Baba.
- Word stress and sentence melody: every word is stressed on the right syllable (a built-in dictionary of several thousand words and names, rules for common endings, and the accent marks you can write in the pronunciation dictionary, such as `telèfon`), and every clause gets a natural contour: a rise at the end of a question, a fall at a full stop, a suspended rise at a comma.
- Josip, Vlado, Detence, Baba and Đedo have a new speech engine: the same word stress and melody as the new voices, a natural tempo, audible p, t, k, b, d and g at high rates, and no more clicks and crackles between sounds. Changing the rate no longer changes the pitch, changing the pitch no longer makes the voice rough, and the derived voices sound like a child, a grandmother and a grandfather instead of a sped-up or slowed-down recording.
- Ten singing voices: Orguljaš, Dalmacija Klapa, Lika Velebit and Bećarac (Zvonko's voice), Trubač, Harmonikaš and Solunac (Stojan's), Sevdalija, Sazlija and Sarajevo Bembaša (Mirsad's). They sing any text to a folk song (Vila Velebita, Kreće se lađa francuska, Kad ja pođoh na Bembašu, the bećarac tune), in any of the three languages; the speech rate sets the tempo, the pitch transposes the song and the inflection level sets the vibrato.
- Two new settings for every voice in the Laprdus Configurator: Inflection level (0% is a monotone, 50% the natural melody, 100% doubles every movement) and Acceleration (a multiplier of the speech rate, 0.5 to 3, so the rate slider reaches a higher or lower top speed; SAPI5 only, NVDA uses its rate boost instead). The new voices accept speech rate and pitch from a quarter to four times the normal value.
- Accent dictionary: a new user dictionary, `accents.json`, corrects the stress of a word for all its forms at once, or of a whole verb. It is edited in the Laprdus Configurator like the other dictionaries (NVDA menu, Tools, Laprdus, Laprdus Configurator, Dictionaries..., dictionary type Accent dictionary), with fields for the entry, its kind (word or verb), its language and a comment; the Configurator checks every entry with the same rules Laprdus reads it by and refuses a malformed one with the reason. It is stored next to the other user dictionaries and loaded with them (see section 5.11 of the user guide).
- Numbers are read in the chosen language (Croatian: *tisuća*, *milijun*; Serbian and Bosnian: *hiljada*, *milion*), by every voice, and one and two agree with the number word (*dvije tisuće*, *dvadeset jedna tisuća*).
- Fixed: entries added to the user spelling and emoji dictionaries had no effect.
- NVDA's Inflection slider sets the inflection level of every voice: 50 is the natural melody, 0 a monotone, 100 twice every movement. The inflection settings of the Laprdus Configurator apply to SAPI5 only. The Acceleration setting is not used under NVDA; NVDA's Rate boost makes every position of the rate slider three times faster, as with other synthesizers (the recorded voices stop at four times the normal rate).
- Settings saved by version 1.0 keep their meaning: an unforced Laprdus rate or pitch, which version 1.0 ignored, stays normal instead of changing the rate NVDA asks for, and intonation switched off in version 1.0 becomes an inflection level of 0% in the Laprdus Configurator (for SAPI5).
- The accent dictionary is loaded from the Laprdus folder with the other user dictionaries and reloaded when the file changes.
- Volume boost: a new switch under the volume in the Laprdus Configurator, off by default, makes the speech louder than the volume slider at 100% allows, for noisy places (traffic, the street) and the phone's earpiece with the speakerphone off: to hear whom you are calling while the call is being set up, or to find the speakerphone button during a call. The speech goes through a strong gain and a peak limiter whose gain moves only between syllables, as the speech amplification of phone systems does: the quiet parts come up by about 8 dB and the loud ones by about 3 dB, nothing is clipped or compressed inside a syllable, the consonants keep their distance from the vowels and the treble above 4 kHz is turned down by 3 dB, so the sound stays clean on a phone's speaker too. NVDA's volume slider still scales the boosted speech. Every voice has it.
- Voice quality: head size, breathiness, roughness and brightness (0-100, 50 = the voice as it is), after the voice-parameter model of a classic formant synthesizer, as four new sliders in NVDA's voice settings and the synth settings ring (the values of the Laprdus Configurator apply to SAPI5 only). Head size and brightness work for the recorded voices too; above 50 the roughness is a rasp, every second cycle of the voice weaker, the breathiness runs from a tense, clear voice at 0 to a whisper at 100, roughness 0 is a perfectly steady voice, and brightness acts on the whole sound, so 0 also softens s and š.
- Sound: the overall balance of Zvonko, Stojan and Mirsad now follows a classic formant synthesizer: more low end, a top that stops at 5.5 kHz as that synthesizer's does, a softer presence region, and every consonant at synthesizer A's level under the vowels (s, š, c and č 15 to 17 dB, f 26, h 20, the bursts of t and p 22 to 24, k louder before a than before i and u), after phone users heard a hiss and piercing consonants at high volume; an utterance that begins with s, š, f or h no longer starts with a tick; the volume boost no longer distorts and keeps the consonants in proportion.

## Version 1.0.0

- Initial release of Laprdus for NVDA: Croatian and Serbian speech with the voices Josip and Vlado and the presets Detence, Baba and Đedo; adjustable rate, pitch and volume.
