# Slovenian

Moved from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-23, unchanged. Claude Code loads this file when Claude reads a file matching the paths above; `CLAUDE.md` lists every topic file.

- **Slovenian** (`src/common/languages/sl/`, `process()` in its `frontend.rs`; "4a. Slovenian" in `docs/formant.md`; the items named below are in that folder: `phonemize.rs`, `lexicon.rs`, `readings.rs`, `clause.rs`, `marks.rs`, `words.rs`, `nouns.rs`, `numerals.rs`, and the generated tables are the files of `tables/`, embedded as statics of the same names): the shared tokenizer and clause structure with rules and a lexicon of its own. Eight vowels: the phones `EH` [ɛ], `OH` [ɔ] (every unstressed and short stressed e, o), `EE` [eː], `OO` [oː] (long stressed close é, ó), `AX` (the schwa written e: veter, pes, misel, the fleeting -er/-el/-en/-ek/-ec), and `W` for the l and v that are [w] (bil, delal, stol, nov, avto; word-initial v before an obstruent is the vowel u: vsak [usak]); lj/nj are l+j, n+j before a vowel and plain l, n otherwise; syllabic r is [ər]; final devoicing (grad [grat], but a preposition keeps its voicing before its host: od orača), regressive voicing assimilation, s/z before š/ž/č, n before k/g/h. Stress is dynamic (long or short, no tones: every word has `Accent::Neutral`): marks in the text (acute = long close, circumflex = long open, grave = short), then the lexicon, then rules (the always-stressed suffixes -írati, -ováti, -éti, -íšče, -ílo, -ízem, -álen, -ljív ...; the syllable before -ica, -ščina, -niti, -cija; otherwise the second-last syllable). The lexicon: the generated `tables/forms.txt` and `tables/stems.txt` (96,000 forms and 80,000 stems covering the word forms of 101,000 Sloleks 3.1 lemmas: every hand-accented one and the automatic ones of frequency 100 or more; front-coded in blocks of 16 and binary-searched in place: no start-up cost, 0.8 MB of text embedded as it is) plus `HAND_ENTRIES` in `lexicon.rs`, which win and hold only words Sloleks lacks and dictionary-backed deviations (about 60 entries since 2026-10-30). The generator reads a homograph by the corpus frequency of the form, not of the lemma (veselím, not the adjective's vesélim), never reads a proper name where a common word has the spelling (sobóta, jeléna; the names go to the tables of names below), makes a fleeting e a schwa (rékel rêkla, začétek začétka, not vídel vídela or a verb's -em), applies `CORRECTIONS` (lemma-level fixes from SSKJ²/SP: dánəs, the first-syllable teens) and then simulates the C++ lookup, hand entries included, over every form, listing whole any form a stem misreads (postavít*, the stem of postavítev, had read postaviti as postavíti) or whose transcribed final [w] it would read [l] (`loses_final_w()`: sledíl* of sledíla had read sledil like the loanwords kanál, hotél; a stem and the ending -l alone is [w] in `phonemize()`; since November 2026, 269 of the 6,000 commonest masculine participles were wrong). The reverse is the table `FINAL_L` (`tables/final_l.txt`, generated: Sloleks's [l] minus the lemmas SSKJ²/SP mark [-u̯], `tools/formant/slovenian_final_w.tsv`), read by `finish_word()`: a word that only looks like a participle keeps [l] (ángel, módul, móbitel, stíl, kábel, ansámbel, Izrael, the genitive plurals sil, škatel), as does a capitalized word in no lexicon (Michael); mísel, vesél, tôpel, žival stay [u̯]; a native listener confirmed the class. Measured on the tagged corpus SUK 1.1 with Sloleks as the reference (`docs/formant.md`, 4a): 99.4 % of the stressed syllables of 297,000 content words right, 98.2 % before. Notation (`Lexicon` in `sl/lexicon.rs`): the word spelled as pronounced with the dictionaries' accented letters (mésto, člôvek, otròk), ə for a schwa, w for an l that is [w], ł for one that stays [l], `*` stem, `|` form list, `spelling=pronunciation`; `'` and `:` as ASCII marks. Users write it in `accents.json` with `"language": "sl"` (an entry without a language is used for Slovenian too when it reads in the notation; Slovenian has no verb entries). Regenerate the table with `python3 tools/formant/slovenian_words.py extract <Sloleks.3.1 dir> forms.tsv 0` (13 GB of XML, about 4 minutes) and `... generate forms.tsv --manual-min 0 --auto-min 100 --form-min 1` (it writes the fifteen tables into `src/common/languages/sl/tables/`, or into a folder named after `forms.tsv`, and reads `HAND_ENTRIES` from `src/common/languages/sl/lexicon.rs` and the SP name files `tools/formant/slovenian_places.tsv` / `slovenian_persons.tsv`, so regenerate after changing them; `--names-report FILE` lists the tables of names for review). **Names** ("Names" in 4a of `docs/formant.md`; a listener reported 2026-10-30 that surnames in -vić/-vic are stressed on the first syllable): a surname in -ović/-ević (-ovič, -ovic ..., any case, `vic_surname()`) is stressed on the syllable before the suffix, the first of three syllables (Pétrović, Túrković, Srébrović) and the second of four (Stojánović, Milóšević, as the hand-accented names of Sloleks), for a capitalized word or any word with ć/đ, ignoring the stems of common words; only a whole form of the table overrides it, and the generator drops Sloleks's network-accented (AUTOMATIC) -vić names. A capitalized word (not all caps, `Capital` in `sl/phonemize.rs`) is looked up first in the generated tables of names (`name_reading()` in `sl/readings.rs`: `NAMES` anywhere, `NAMES_INSIDE` not at the head of a clause or after an opening quote): the hand-accented Sloleks names that a common word is spelled like (Múrska Sóbota/sobóta, Róman/román; at the head only a name three times as frequent as the common word and not the second word of a place, so "Sobota je lep dan" is the day; never adjectives more frequent than the name, Stare/Bele, nor `COMMON_CAPITALIZED`: v Boga, Svetega Duha), and the places and persons of Slovenski pravopis 2001 harvested from Fran (1,003 places, 167 persons; declined by `place_forms()`: Bôhinj Bohínja, Trbôvlje Trbôvelj, Litíja, Sôlkan; SP wins over Sloleks except where SP accents the word otherwise in another entry or the spelling is a form of another Sloleks name). Inside a clause a capitalized word takes a stem only with a declension or possessive ending, also after one consonant (`declension_ending()`: Koróšca, Cánkarjev; not Ferd*+olenc).
